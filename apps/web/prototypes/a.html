      Films, series and catch-up.

      Live TV is the front door, not the whole house. Browse films and series from your source in the same app, grouped by genre, and go back in time on channels where your source offers catch-up.

      On the sofa and at the desk.

      One account, two screens. The Fire TV app is built for a remote and a distance; the Windows app for a mouse and a keyboard. Sign in on the TV with a short code instead of typing a password with arrow keys.

      1. On the TV: open Link TV and read the code.
      2. On your computer: enter the code on the Link TV page.
      3. Done: the TV is signed in to the same account.

      Bring your own sources.

      Testcard ships no channels. It is a player. We don't sell, host or recommend channels or providers, and there is nothing to subscribe to here.

      If you already have a source you are entitled to watch, add it:

      • an Xtream login (server, username, password), or
      • an M3U playlist URL.

      FAQ

      Where do the channels come from?

      From you. Testcard has no channel list of its own. Without a source it is an empty guide.

      Will it work with my provider?

      If your provider gives you an Xtream login or an M3U playlist, it should. Provider quality varies, and we can't fix a stream your provider doesn't deliver.

      Is this legal?

      Testcard is a player, like a media player. What you watch with it is between you and your provider. Use sources you are entitled to use.
