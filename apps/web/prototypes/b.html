A player. Not a provider.

Testcard plays the Xtream and M3U sources you already pay for, on Windows and Fire TV. It ships no channels, no playlists and no recommendations. It just makes what you have watchable.

Simulcast

On air on two screens

Set up your sources once. Your TV shows a short code, you type it on the computer you already use, and the sofa screen has the same setup.

  1. On the TV, choose Link TV and read the code.
  2. On any browser, open /link.
  3. Enter the code. That's it.

Viewer questions

Questions at the desk

Does Testcard come with channels?

No. Testcard is only a player. You bring an Xtream login or an M3U playlist from a provider you already use.

Where do I get a source?

From your IPTV provider. Testcard does not sell, recommend or link to any.

Which devices does it run on?

Windows and Fire TV. There is no phone app yet.
