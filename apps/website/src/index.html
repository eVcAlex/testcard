IPTV player · Windows & Fire TV

A dark, watermark-free IPTV player.

Xtream Codes and M3U sources, live TV with a guide, films and series, and one account that keeps your sources, favourites and where you left off in step across Windows and Fire TV.

Why it exists

Built as a free alternative to the paywalled extras of commercial IPTV players: dark mode, no watermark and playlist auto-refresh are just there.

Features

Everything a player should do, quietly.

Xtream and M3U

Live channels, films and series, with catch-up where your provider keeps it.

Tidy names

Channel and category names cleaned up whatever the provider’s style: “UK| ʙʙᴄ ᴏɴᴇ ᴴᴰ” becomes “BBC One HD”.

A real TV guide

XMLTV guide grid, plus now and next with a progress bar on every channel.

Fast at any size

Stored locally in SQLite, so it stays quick at tens of thousands of channels.

One account, every screen

Sources, favourites, Continue watching and watched state stay in step across devices.

Profiles

Up to four people per account on Fire TV, each with their own Continue watching, favourites and progress.

Plays what browsers can’t

On Windows, playback runs through mpv, so HEVC video and E-AC-3 audio just work.

Falls back by itself

On Fire TV, a channel that won’t start tries its other feeds and qualities, 4K to HD.

Refresh on a schedule

Refresh by hand or every 6, 12 or 24 hours. Favourites survive a provider’s renumbering.

Hide what you don’t watch

Hidden categories and channels stay out of every list, on every device.

Captions your way

On Fire TV, restyle captions’ size, colour and background, in the language you choose.

Updates itself

New versions are offered in the app, with what changed.

Platforms

Windows and Fire TV.

Windows

64-bit installer. Sources are added and edited here, playback through embedded mpv, updates itself. The installer isn’t code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may warn on first install.

Fire TV

Built for the remote: Home, Movies, Series, Live TV and Search, with a TV guide grid. Sign in and your sources, favourites and progress arrive. Installed by sideloading; updates itself after that.

Phones aren’t supported yet: the layout is drawn for a TV.

Testcard ships no content.

Testcard is a player. It includes no channels, playlists, streams or subscriptions, and Evicted doesn’t sell, recommend or link to any. It works only with sources you add yourself, and you are responsible for using only services and content you are legally entitled to access. Your provider logins stay on your device in the system’s secure store and only travel between your devices encrypted with a key derived from your account password.
