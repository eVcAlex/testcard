How it works

Three steps, and the second one is done for you.

  1. Add a source

    On Windows, paste an M3U or XMLTV address, or enter your Xtream host and login. A pasted Xtream get.php link works too. You can add as many sources as you have.

  2. Testcard tidies it up

    Providers ship the same channel five times at different qualities, under names like “UK| ʙʙᴄ ᴏɴᴇ ᴴᴰ”. Testcard groups those into one channel, cleans the names and categories, and matches the guide to them.

  3. Watch anywhere

    Sign in on your Fire TV and your sources, favourites and progress are already there. Start an episode on one screen and Continue watching has it on the other.

Fire TV

Built for the remote: Home, Movies, Series, Live TV and Search, with a guide grid on Live TV. Sign in with a code and your sources arrive.

Installed by sideloading during early access, and it updates itself after that.

Phones aren’t supported yet: the layout is drawn for a TV.

Testcard ships no content.

Testcard is a player. It includes no channels, playlists, streams or subscriptions, and Evicted doesn’t sell, recommend or link to any. It works only with sources you add yourself, and you are responsible for using only services and content you are legally entitled to access.

Questions

Does it come with channels?

No. Testcard is only the player. You bring your own sources, from services and content you are entitled to use.

Which sources work?

Xtream Codes accounts, and M3U or M3U8 playlists, with an optional XMLTV address for the guide.

Is it free?

Free during early access. If that changes, you’ll hear about it before it does.

Is my data safe?

Your provider logins stay on your device, in the system’s secure store. They only travel between your devices encrypted with a key derived from your account password.

Does it work on phones?

Not yet. The Fire TV layout is drawn for a remote and a big screen, and on Windows it is a desktop app.

Why does Windows warn me about the installer?

The installer isn’t code-signed yet, so SmartScreen shows a warning on first install. That is expected during early access.
